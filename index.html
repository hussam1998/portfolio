Teaching Approach

A method built on close reading, not guesswork

Four steps, repeated every lesson — the same discipline used when a client needs a translation to be exactly right.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A short conversation to place your real level and pin down what "fluent" means for you specifically.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A lesson sequence built around your goals — conversation, exam, business, or reading classical texts.

  3. 03

    Practice

    Real material — your emails, articles, or dialogue — worked through together, out loud.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Written feedback after each session, marked up the way I would mark a client's document.
